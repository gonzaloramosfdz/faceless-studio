Privacy policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Who uses this app

Faceless Studio is a tool run by the owner of one YouTube channel to publish that channel's own videos. Only the channel owner signs in. It is not available to the public and does not have other users.

What the app accesses

With the owner's permission, the app uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos to the owner's channel, change the visibility of those videos, and read their view, like and comment counts.

What is stored

The app stores the owner's Google authorization token as an encrypted secret in the project's GitHub repository settings. It stores the IDs and public statistics of the videos it uploaded. It does not collect, store or share personal data about viewers or anyone else, and it does not use cookies or analytics on this site.

Sharing

No data is sold or shared with third parties. Videos and their descriptions are sent to YouTube and Instagram for publication, which is the purpose of the tool.

Removing access

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions can be raised at github.com/gonzaloramosfdz/faceless-studio/issues.
